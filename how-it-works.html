How it works

Continuous verification, not point in time audits.

Verysset transforms static verification into a continuous, measurable and traceable process, giving rise to a real time verifiable digital twin of the asset.

Step 01

Structured ingestion

Physical, legal and documentary evidence associated with the asset is ingested continuously.

Step 02

Continuous verification

Data integrity and coherence are verified persistently over time.

Step 03

Custody & scoring

A verifiable asset history is custodied with a dynamic trust score.

Result

A living digital twin of the asset, continuously updated, that remains observable, auditable and governable throughout its operational lifecycle.
